FAQ

Is this legal?

The Cannabis for Private Purposes Act, 2024 allows adults to use and grow cannabis privately, but it does not allow public sales. Smokey Dreamz runs as a private members' club: we don't sell to the public, don't sell online and don't deliver. Members share in the club and make contributions.

What ID do I need?

A valid South African ID, driver's licence or passport. Every member must be 18 or older, and we check ID when you sign up.

Any advice on edibles?

Start low and go slow. Edibles can take 30 minutes to 2 hours to kick in, and they last longer than smoking. Take a small amount, wait, and don't mix with alcohol. Ask us if you're unsure.

Can I smoke in public?

No. Smoking cannabis in public is not allowed. Enjoy it privately, never drive after using, and keep it away from children.

Do you deliver or sell online?

No. Everything happens in person, inside the club, for members only.
