    FAQ

    Good questions

    Is this legal?

    Under the Cannabis for Private Purposes Act, 2024, adults may use, possess and grow cannabis for private purposes. Smokey Dreamz operates as a private members' club: we don't sell to the public, and only registered adult members can take part. The law in this area is still developing, and we follow it closely.

    Can I just walk in?

    You need to be a registered member first. Applying takes a few minutes, and you finish signing up on your first visit with your ID.

    What do I need to bring?

    A valid South African ID, driver's licence or passport. We check the age of every member.

    I'm new to edibles. Where do I start?

    Start with a low dose, like a single 5mg gummy, and wait at least 90 minutes before taking more. The team can point you to beginner-friendly options.

    Can I smoke in public afterwards?

    No. Smoking cannabis in public or around children is not allowed. Enjoy responsibly, in private, and never drive under the influence.
