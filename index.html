FAQ

Good questions

Is this legal?

Under the Cannabis for Private Purposes Act, 2024, adults may use, possess and grow cannabis for private purposes. Smokey Dreamz operates as a private members' club: we don't sell to the public, and only registered adult members can take part. The law in this area is still developing, and we follow it closely.

Can I just walk in and buy something?

No. We're a private club, so you need to be a registered member first. Applying takes a few minutes, and you can finish signing up on your first visit with your ID.

What do I need to bring?

A valid South African ID, driver's licence or passport. We check the age of every member.

Do you deliver?

Not at the moment. Members collect in person at the club.

Can I smoke in public afterwards?

No. Smoking cannabis in public, or around children, is not allowed. Please enjoy responsibly, in private, and never drive under the influence.
